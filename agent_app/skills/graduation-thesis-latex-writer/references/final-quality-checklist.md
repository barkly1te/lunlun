# Full Thesis Final Check

## When to read this file

Read this checklist whenever the task is larger than an outline. Use it at the end of a full chapter, a multi-chapter draft, or a near-final thesis.

## Structure and continuity

- The Chinese abstract, English abstract, body chapters, and conclusion follow the same main story.
- Chapter, section, and subsection responsibilities are clear.
- Each chapter answers the question it is supposed to answer.
- The conclusion is supported by the actual body content and does not overclaim.
- Closing materials such as references, appendices, acknowledgements, and TOC are present where required.

## Terminology and abbreviations

- A single term keeps the same name throughout the thesis.
- Abbreviations are expanded only at the first formal appearance in the body.
- Already expanded abbreviations are not incorrectly treated as first appearance again.
- Chinese term, English term, and abbreviation match each other correctly.

## Figures, tables, and equations

- Every figure and table is introduced in the body before or at first appearance.
- Figure captions, table captions, and supporting explanation lines are complete.
- Figure explanation appears once, not both before and after the figure unless clearly necessary.
- Placeholder figures clearly state what the author still needs to add.
- `label` names are unique and all `\ref{}` calls resolve cleanly.

## Citations and bibliography

- Every citation used in the body is real and verifiable.
- The bibliography contains only items that are actually cited.
- Figure sources, table sources, and data sources are all documented where needed.
- External figure-source notes are reflected in the bibliography or the equivalent citation system.
- There is no orphaned `[x]` or `\cite{}` without a matching bibliography entry.

## Claim discipline and prose quality

- No obvious template filler, slogan-like prose, or fake fullness remains.
- Performance claims and innovation claims are supported by evidence.
- Paragraphs roughly follow `problem -> explanation -> conclusion`.
- Engineering description is not pretending to be theoretical contribution.

## Compilation and cleanup

- The thesis compiles successfully.
- TOC, figure numbering, table numbering, and references are correct.
- No `TODO`, empty placeholder, or demo text remains by accident.
- No fake author data, fake metrics, or illustrative junk text remains.
- Page headers, page breaks, and chapter-opening pages are still intact.
